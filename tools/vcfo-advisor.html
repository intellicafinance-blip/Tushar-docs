Knowledge Base

Key Financial Ratios Analyzed by VCFO

Understanding these ratios helps you prepare a stronger loan application and improve approval chances.

FOIR

Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio

Measures the percentage of your income going towards fixed obligations like EMIs, rent, and insurance premiums. Banks typically prefer FOIR to be below 50-60%. A lower FOIR indicates better repayment capacity and improves your loan eligibility.

DSCR

Debt Service Coverage Ratio

Indicates the ability of a business to service its debt from net operating income. A DSCR of greater than 1.5 is considered healthy by most banks. It means the business generates 1.5 times more income than required to cover its debt obligations.

Debt-Equity Ratio

Leverage Indicator

Compares total debt with shareholders' equity to assess financial leverage. Banks prefer a debt-equity ratio of less than 3:1. A lower ratio suggests the business is not overly dependent on borrowed funds and has adequate owner investment.

Net Profit Margin

Profitability Indicator

Represents the percentage of revenue that remains as profit after all expenses. Higher is better - it demonstrates the business is efficiently managed and has good pricing power. Banks see this as a sign of sustainable operations.

EBITDA

Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation & Amortization

A key indicator of business health and operational profitability. It strips out non-operational factors to show core business performance. Banks use EBITDA to assess the true cash-generating ability of a business before financing costs.

About LAP

What is Loan Against Property?

A Loan Against Property (LAP) is a secured loan where you pledge your existing residential or commercial property as collateral to avail a high-value loan. It is one of the most cost-effective ways to raise large funds for personal or business needs, as the interest rates are significantly lower than unsecured loans.

LAP can be availed by both salaried and self-employed individuals. The loan amount is determined based on the market value of the property, with banks typically offering up to 65% of the property's current market value (Loan-to-Value ratio).

Intellica Financial Services partners with leading PSU and private sector banks to bring you the best LAP deals with competitive rates, flexible tenures, and quick processing. Our expert advisors guide you through every step, from property valuation to final disbursement.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Both residential and commercial property accepted as collateral
  • Overdraft (OD) facility available for flexible fund utilization
  • No restriction on end-use of funds - use for business, education, wedding, or medical expenses
  • Property types eligible: Residential flat, independent house, commercial property, industrial land
  • Balance transfer option to shift existing LAP at lower rates
  • Tax benefits available under Section 37(1) when used for business purposes
  • Lower interest rates compared to personal loans and business loans
Eligibility

Who Can Apply for LAP?

Check if you meet the basic eligibility criteria for Loan Against Property.

Age Criteria

Minimum age: 25 years
Maximum age at loan maturity: 65 years (salaried) / 70 years (self-employed)

Minimum Income

Salaried: Rs. 30,000/month
Self-employed: Rs. 3 Lakh/year net profit (as per ITR)

CIBIL Score

Minimum credit score of 675+ required. Higher scores unlock better interest rates and higher LTV ratios.

Property Ownership

Property must be self-owned with clear title and free from any legal disputes or encumbrances.

Business Vintage

Self-employed applicants must have a minimum 3 years of business vintage with ITR filings. Salaried need 2 years of work experience.

Applicant Type

Available for salaried employees, self-employed professionals, and self-employed businesspersons. NRIs also eligible with select banks.

Documentation

Documents Required for LAP

Keep the following documents ready to ensure a smooth and fast loan processing experience.

KYC Documents

  • PAN Card (mandatory for all applicants and co-applicants)
  • Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport / Driving License
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)
  • Address proof (Utility bill / Aadhaar / Passport)
  • Signature verification from existing banker

Income Proof Documents

  • Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 3 years with computation
  • GST returns for last 12 months (for self-employed)
  • Bank statements for last 12 months (all operative accounts)
  • Salary slips for last 6 months (for salaried applicants)
  • Form 16 / Form 26AS for last 2 years (for salaried)
  • Audited balance sheet and P&L statement for 3 years (if applicable)

Property Documents

  • Original title deed (sale deed / conveyance deed)
  • Complete chain of documents (previous sale deeds / link documents)
  • Approved building plan and commencement certificate
  • NOC from society / builder / housing authority
  • Property tax receipts (paid up to date)
  • Occupation certificate / Completion certificate
  • Latest property valuation report (if available)

Business Documents (Self-Employed)

  • Business registration certificate / Partnership deed / MOA & AOA
  • GST registration certificate
  • Trade license / Shop & Establishment certificate
  • Proof of business address
  • Business profile or company introduction
  • MSME / Udyam registration certificate (if applicable)

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Loan Against Property.

A Home Loan is used specifically to purchase a new property or construct a house, while a Loan Against Property (LAP) is a loan taken by pledging an already owned property as collateral. In LAP, the funds can be used for any purpose -- business expansion, education, medical expenses, wedding, or debt consolidation. LAP typically has slightly higher interest rates than home loans but offers more flexibility in fund utilization.
Banks accept several property types as collateral for LAP including: residential flats and apartments, independent houses and bungalows, commercial office spaces and shops, industrial property and land (select banks), and plots of land in approved layouts. The property must have a clear title, should be free from litigation, and must be located within the bank's approved geographical areas. Agricultural land is generally not accepted for LAP.
Yes, many banks offer an overdraft (OD) facility under Loan Against Property. With the OD facility, you get a sanctioned credit limit against your property, and you only pay interest on the amount you actually use. This is highly beneficial for business owners who need flexible access to funds. You can withdraw and repay multiple times within the sanctioned limit without reapplying. The interest rate for OD facility may be slightly higher than term loan rates, but you save significantly by paying interest only on the utilized amount.
The typical processing time for a Loan Against Property is 7 to 15 working days from the date of complete document submission. This includes property verification and legal opinion (3-5 days), technical valuation of the property (2-3 days), credit assessment and sanction (2-3 days), and final documentation and disbursement (2-3 days). At Intellica Financial Services, we work closely with our banking partners to expedite the process and ensure you receive your funds as quickly as possible.
Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your LAP. As per RBI guidelines, for floating rate loans taken by individual borrowers, banks cannot charge any prepayment or foreclosure penalty. However, for fixed rate loans or loans taken by non-individual entities (companies, firms), banks may levy a prepayment charge of 2-4% on the outstanding principal. Part-prepayment is also allowed by most banks, which can significantly reduce your overall interest burden and loan tenure. We recommend making part-prepayments whenever you have surplus funds to save on interest costs.
When you take a Loan Against Property, the bank creates a mortgage on your property by holding the original property documents as security. However, you retain full possession and usage rights of the property. You can continue to live in it, rent it out, or use it for business operations. The only restriction is that you cannot sell or transfer the property without the bank's written consent (NOC). Once you fully repay the loan, the bank releases the mortgage and returns all original property documents to you.

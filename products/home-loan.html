Everything You Need to Know About Home Loans

A home loan is a secured loan offered by banks and financial institutions to help you purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property. The property itself serves as collateral, making it one of the most affordable borrowing options available.

At Intellica Financial Services, we partner with over 15 leading banks across India to bring you the most competitive home loan rates and terms. Our expert loan advisors guide you through every step -- from choosing the right lender to final disbursement -- ensuring a smooth and transparent experience.

Whether you are a first-time homebuyer, looking to build your dream home from scratch, or planning to renovate your existing property, we have the right solution for you.

Types of Home Loans We Offer

  • Home Purchase Loan -- Finance the purchase of a new or resale residential property with up to 90% of the property value.
  • Home Construction Loan -- Build your dream home on a plot you already own. Funds are disbursed in stages as construction progresses.
  • Home Renovation / Improvement Loan -- Upgrade, repair, or renovate your existing home with affordable financing options.
  • Plot Purchase Loan -- Buy a residential plot and plan your construction later. Available with competitive rates and flexible tenure.
  • Balance Transfer -- Transfer your existing home loan to a bank offering a lower interest rate and save significantly on EMIs.

Eligibility

Home Loan Eligibility Criteria

Check if you meet the basic eligibility requirements to apply for a home loan through Intellica Financial Services.

Age

Minimum 21 years at the time of application. Maximum 65 years at loan maturity.

Minimum Income

Salaried: Minimum Rs. 25,000 per month. Self-employed: Minimum Rs. 3 Lakh annual income.

CIBIL Score

Minimum credit score of 650 required. A score of 750+ improves your chances of getting better rates.

Employment Stability

Salaried: Minimum 2 years of work experience with at least 1 year in current job. Self-employed: 3+ years in business.

Property Type

Residential property (flat, house, villa) from an approved builder or in an approved project. Resale properties also eligible.

Co-Applicant

Adding a co-applicant (spouse, parent) can increase your eligible loan amount. Co-owner of property must be a co-applicant.

Documentation

Documents Required

Keep the following documents ready for a smooth and quick home loan application process.

KYC Documents

  • PAN Card (mandatory for all applicants)
  • Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs
  • Current address proof (utility bill, bank statement, or rent agreement)

Income Proof

  • Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips and Form 16
  • Salaried: Employment certificate or appointment letter
  • Self-employed: Last 3 years IT returns with computation of income
  • Self-employed: Profit & Loss account and Balance Sheet (CA certified)
  • Business registration / GST certificate (if applicable)

Property Documents

  • Sale agreement / allotment letter from the builder
  • Title deed and chain of previous ownership documents
  • Approved building plan and commencement certificate
  • Occupancy certificate (for ready-to-move-in properties)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the housing society (if applicable)

Bank Statements

  • Last 6 months bank statements of the primary salary/business account
  • Existing loan account statements (if any)
  • Proof of down payment / own contribution
  • Credit card statements for the last 3 months (if applicable)

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about home loans.

Banks typically finance up to 80-90% of the property value (known as the Loan-to-Value or LTV ratio). This means you need to arrange a minimum down payment of 10-20% of the property value from your own funds. For loans up to Rs. 30 Lakh, the maximum LTV is 90%. For loans between Rs. 30 Lakh and Rs. 75 Lakh, it is 80%, and for loans above Rs. 75 Lakh, it is 75% as per RBI guidelines.
Home loan eligibility is primarily based on your income, existing obligations (EMIs), age, credit score, and employment stability. Banks typically allow an EMI-to-income ratio of 50-60%, meaning your total EMIs (including the proposed home loan EMI) should not exceed 50-60% of your net monthly income. A higher CIBIL score (750+), stable employment, and lower existing debt improve your eligibility significantly.
Yes, home loans offer significant tax benefits under the Income Tax Act. Under Section 80C, you can claim a deduction of up to Rs. 1.5 Lakh per year on the principal repayment. Under Section 24(b), you can claim a deduction of up to Rs. 2 Lakh per year on the interest paid for a self-occupied property. For first-time homebuyers, an additional deduction of up to Rs. 1.5 Lakh is available under Section 80EEA (subject to conditions). If the property is let out, there is no upper limit on the interest deduction under Section 24(b).
A fixed interest rate remains constant throughout the loan tenure, giving you predictable EMIs. A floating interest rate is linked to the bank's external benchmark (like the RBI repo rate) and changes periodically based on market conditions. Most banks in India currently offer only floating rate home loans. Floating rates are generally lower than fixed rates and come with zero prepayment charges. We recommend floating rates for most borrowers as they offer better flexibility and are currently at historic lows.
With Intellica Financial Services, the home loan process is streamlined for speed. Typically, you can expect a sanction letter within 48-72 hours of submitting all required documents. The complete disbursement process, including legal and technical verification of the property, takes around 7-15 working days. We assign a dedicated relationship manager who ensures smooth coordination between you and the bank, minimizing delays at every step.
Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your home loan at any time. As per RBI guidelines, banks cannot charge any prepayment or foreclosure penalty on floating rate home loans. This means you can make partial prepayments or pay off the entire outstanding amount without any additional charges. For fixed rate loans, some banks may charge a foreclosure fee of 2-3%. We always recommend making prepayments whenever possible, as even small additional payments can significantly reduce your total interest outgo and loan tenure.

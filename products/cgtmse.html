About the Scheme

What is CGTMSE?

The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) was established by the Government of India (Ministry of MSME) and SIDBI in 2000. The scheme provides credit guarantee to lending institutions against the default risk in loans extended to eligible micro and small enterprises (MSEs).

Under this scheme, banks and financial institutions can extend collateral-free and third-party guarantee-free credit to MSEs. The Trust covers the risk of default, encouraging lenders to offer loans to small businesses that might otherwise lack adequate collateral to secure traditional financing.

The scheme covers enterprises engaged in manufacturing, services, and trading activities. Both new and existing enterprises are eligible, making it a powerful tool for entrepreneurs looking to start or expand their businesses without the burden of pledging personal or business assets.

Key Features

  • No collateral or third-party guarantee required for loans up to Rs. 5 Crore
  • Covers both term loans and working capital facilities
  • Available for new and existing enterprises in manufacturing, services, and trading
  • One-time guarantee fee as low as 0.37% of the sanctioned credit facility
  • Guarantee cover ranging from 75% to 85% of the sanctioned loan amount

Eligibility

Who Can Apply for CGTMSE Loan?

Check if your enterprise meets the eligibility criteria for collateral-free funding under the CGTMSE scheme.

MSE Classification

Manufacturing units with investment up to Rs. 10 Crore and services units with investment up to Rs. 5 Crore in plant & machinery or equipment.

New or Existing Enterprise

Both new startups and existing businesses are eligible under the scheme. The enterprise must be engaged in manufacturing, services, or trading activities.

Business Structure

Proprietorship firms, partnership firms, LLPs, private limited companies, and other business entities are all eligible to apply for CGTMSE loans.

Credit Score

A minimum CIBIL score of 650 or above is generally required. A higher credit score improves your chances of approval and may offer better terms.

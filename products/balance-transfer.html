What is a Balance Transfer / Loan Takeover?

A balance transfer (also called a loan takeover) is the process of moving your existing loan from one bank or financial institution to another that offers a lower interest rate. This allows you to benefit from reduced EMIs, lower total interest outgo, and potentially better service from the new lender.

Banks are constantly revising their interest rates. If you took a loan a few years ago at a higher rate, chances are you can now get a significantly lower rate by transferring your loan. Intellica Financial Services helps you identify the best opportunities and manages the entire transfer process seamlessly.

When Should You Consider a Balance Transfer?

A balance transfer makes sense when one or more of the following conditions apply:

  • Your existing interest rate is higher than current market rates (even a 0.5% difference matters)
  • You are getting poor service or support from your current bank
  • You need additional top-up funds over and above your existing loan
  • You have a significant remaining loan tenure (5+ years) where savings compound
  • Your credit score has improved since you originally took the loan

How is this calculated?
EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P = Principal, r = Monthly Rate, n = Total Months. The savings shown are the difference between your current and new EMI multiplied by the remaining tenure.

Documentation

Documents Required for Balance Transfer

Keep these documents ready for a smooth and quick balance transfer process. Our team will guide you through every step.

From Existing Bank

  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) from existing bank
  • Loan account statement (from disbursement to date)
  • Repayment track record / EMI payment history
  • Foreclosure letter with outstanding amount
  • List of original property documents held by the bank

Property Documents

  • Sale deed / Agreement to sale
  • Property registration documents
  • Approved building plan and completion certificate
  • Encumbrance certificate (latest)
  • Property tax receipts (paid up to date)

Income Proofs

  • Latest 6 months salary slips (for salaried)
  • Latest 2 years ITR with computation (for self-employed)
  • Latest 12 months bank statements
  • Form 16 / Form 26AS (for salaried)
  • Business financials - P&L, Balance Sheet (for self-employed)

Identity & Address Proof

  • PAN Card (mandatory)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport / Voter ID / Driving License
  • Latest utility bill or rent agreement (for address)
  • Passport-size photographs

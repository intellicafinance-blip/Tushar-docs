Types of Business Loans

Understanding the right type of business loan is crucial for optimal financial planning. Our experts help you choose the best structure based on your business profile, cash flow, and growth objectives.

Secured Business Loan -- Avail higher loan amounts at lower interest rates by pledging property, fixed deposits, or other assets as collateral. Ideal for businesses looking for substantial capital at competitive rates. Loan amounts can go up to Rs. 50 Crore depending on collateral value.

Unsecured Business Loan -- No collateral needed. Perfect for businesses that need quick funds without pledging assets. Based on your business financials and creditworthiness, you can avail loans up to Rs. 75 Lakh with minimal documentation.

Working Capital Loan (CC/OD Facility) -- Cash Credit and Overdraft facilities to manage day-to-day business operations. Pay interest only on the amount utilized. Ideal for businesses with seasonal demand fluctuations or those needing revolving credit.

Term Loan for Expansion -- Long-term financing for business expansion, purchasing machinery, setting up new branches, or acquiring another business. Structured EMI repayment with tenures up to 7 years.

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

Check if you meet the basic eligibility requirements for a business loan. Our experts can help even if your profile needs improvement.

Business Vintage

Minimum 3 years of business operations with a proven track record of stability and growth.

Minimum Turnover

Annual business turnover of at least Rs. 50 Lakh as reflected in financial statements and GST returns.

CIBIL Score 700+

A credit score of 700 or above for the primary applicant and all co-applicants or guarantors.

GST Registered

Active GST registration with regular filing of returns. Reflects compliance and genuine business operations.

Profitable for 2+ Years

Business should have reported net profit in the last 2 consecutive financial years as per ITR filings.

Age 25 - 65 Years

Primary applicant should be between 25 and 65 years of age at the time of loan maturity.

Documentation

Documents Required

Keep these documents ready for a smooth and quick loan application process. Our team will guide you through every step.

KYC Documents

  • PAN Card of business entity and all partners/directors
  • Aadhaar Card of all partners/directors
  • Passport-size photographs of all applicants
  • Address proof (utility bill, passport, or voter ID)

Business Proof

  • GST Registration Certificate
  • Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSMEs)
  • Trade License / Shop Establishment Certificate
  • Partnership Deed / MOA & AOA / LLP Agreement
  • Business continuity proof (ITR or GST filings for 3 years)

Financial Documents

  • Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 3 years
  • Bank statements for the last 12 months (all business accounts)
  • Audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss statement (3 years)
  • GST returns for the last 12 months
  • Projected financials (for new projects/expansion)

Collateral Documents (If Secured)

  • Property title deed and chain of documents
  • Approved building plan and completion certificate
  • Property tax receipts (latest)
  • Valuation report from empanelled valuer
  • Fixed Deposit receipts (if pledging FD as security)

A Cash Credit (CC) is a revolving credit facility where you get a sanctioned limit and can withdraw funds as needed, paying interest only on the amount utilized. It is ideal for managing day-to-day working capital needs. A Term Loan, on the other hand, is a lump-sum amount disbursed upfront that is repaid through fixed EMIs over a predetermined tenure. Term loans are better suited for business expansion, equipment purchase, or one-time capital expenditures.

For secured business loans and higher amounts, the process typically takes 7 to 15 working days as it involves property valuation, legal verification, and additional due diligence.
